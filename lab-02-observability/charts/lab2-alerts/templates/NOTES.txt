Check receiver rollout and received notifications:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deployment/lab2-alert-receiver --timeout=120s
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs deployment/lab2-alert-receiver -f

Open each monitoring port-forward in a separate terminal:
  kubectl -n monitoring port-forward service/monitoring-prometheus 19090:9090
  kubectl -n monitoring port-forward service/monitoring-alertmanager 19093:9093
